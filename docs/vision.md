# Vision

## Problem
At hackathons, the first question is always "what shall we build?". There are endless directions, but nothing pushes your thinking past the first obvious ideas, so it's hard to go deep enough to find something genuinely worth building.

## What Floppy Thoughts is
A brainstorming board that gets the ball rolling. Type your thoughts, link them, and get random starting notes from a vending machine full of shrimp whenever you want a push. When you're stuck, ask for a nudge: a word or a question, never an answer. The ideas stay yours.

## Principles
- **Autonomy first:** nudges are words or questions, never finished ideas. No LLM.
- **Push past the obvious:** the aim isn't to avoid anything that already exists, it's to get beyond basic clones.
- **Calm board:** low stimulus, so the visuals don't steer what people think.
- **Whimsy at the edges:** the shrimp (a nod to coders' hunched "shrimp posture"), pixel art and easter eggs live in the frame and the machine, not on the board.
- **No scores, timers or limits.**
- **Fun over money:** ideas can make money, but that isn't the point.
- **£0 to run:** free APIs and free hosting.
- **Secure and private by design.**

## Phases
| Phase | Scope |
|---|---|
| Now | Solo boards in the browser: typing and linking thoughts, the vending machine, nudges, tidy, favourites, "this is the one" summaries, a terminal with commands |
| Next | Accounts, profiles and settings, sharing boards with view or edit access |
| Later | Live group boards, pixel-art mascot and easter eggs, phone layout |
